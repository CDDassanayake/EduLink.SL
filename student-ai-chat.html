What careers can I pursue with A/L Physical Science stream?
With A/L Physical Science (Maths, Physics, Chemistry/ICT), you have excellent options in Sri Lanka:

🎓 Engineering — University of Moratuwa (most competitive), University of Peradeniya, SLIIT. High z-score required.

💻 Information Technology — BSc IT at UoM or SLIIT. Great choice if you enjoy programming.

⚗️ Physical Sciences / Research — University of Colombo, Peradeniya. Good path into academia or R&D.

🏗️ Architecture — UoM or SLIDA. Combines technical + creative skills.

📡 Telecommunications — Growing sector in SL, especially with 5G expansion.

I'm interested in IT. What z-score do I need for UoM BSc IT?
For UoM BSc (Hons) IT (Physical Science stream), you typically need:

• Z-score: 1.8 – 2.2+ (varies by year, very competitive)
• 2025 cutoff was approximately 1.95 for district merit
• Minimum: 3 passes with at least a C in Combined Maths or ICT

Tips to improve your z-score:
1. Focus heavily on Combined Maths — it has the highest weighting
2. Book a verified tutor for past paper practice (Dr. Rohan Silva on EduLink has a 99 merit score)
3. Aim for A grades in all 3 subjects
